- `@sportsdataverse/sporty` ports geometry and the surface-dimensions data of **sportyR** (Ross Drucker) and
  **sportypy**. Those packages are published under GPL-3; by agreement between the SportsDataverse maintainers
  (recorded 2026-10-05, spec decision J3) the ported material is distributed here under MIT.
- `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot` re-shards the team index curated in **sdvplot** (Python, MIT) and reads the

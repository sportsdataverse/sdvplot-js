New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/svelte`, the first Svelte layer: Svelte 5 components for LayerChart charts, each a child of the chart's `<Svg>` layer. `LogoMark` draws team logos (or wordmarks) and `HeadshotMark` ESPN headshots at the rows' scaled (x, y), sized as a fraction of the plot area's height (a team or player with no image draws nothing and warns once); `SurfaceBackdrop` paints a team-coloured court, field or rink under the marks in the chart's x/y units; `teamColorPalette` gives one colour per team for the series, keeping 3:1 contrast against a `background` for dark mode and every team distinct (CIE76 ΔE of 10 or more). The components ship as `.svelte` source under the `svelte` export condition (built with `@sveltejs/package`) and load layerchart, a new optional peer (>= 2.0, with `svelte` >= 5), only when they render: importing `./svelte` never needs it, and a missing peer throws an install message from the component that needs it.

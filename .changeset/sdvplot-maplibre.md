"@sportsdataverse/sdvplot": minor
---

New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/maplibre`: MapLibre GL JS sources and layers for teams, as plain style JSON. `logoSymbols(rows, o)` returns a GeoJSON source of one point per row (longitude and latitude columns; the feature id is the row's index) and a `symbol` layer drawing each team's logo, with the images the layer needs; team values may be numbers or strings, and a team with no logo or a missing coordinate is left out with one warning. `addLogoImages(map, ...sets)` loads each logo into the map once (`loadImage` and `addImage`, drawn `size` px tall), skipping images the map already has and warning once for one that fails to load. `routeLines(rows, o)` returns a source of two-point lines and a `line` layer in team colours; with `background`, every colour keeps 3:1 contrast and aims to stay distinct through the shared team palette. `basemap("light" | "dark")` returns OpenFreeMap's keyless `positron` or `dark` style URL, its credit and its background colour. `drawnMarks` lists the logos drawn. It never imports MapLibre; `maplibre-gl` (>= 6.14.0) and `@deck.gl/mapbox` (>= 9.4.0, for `/deckgl`'s layers over a MapLibre map) are new optional peers.

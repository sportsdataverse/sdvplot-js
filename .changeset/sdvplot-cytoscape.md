"@sportsdataverse/sdvplot": minor

New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/cytoscape`: passing networks for Cytoscape.js, as plain elements and stylesheet JSON. `passNetwork(rows, o)` returns `{ elements, style }`: one node per player and one directed edge per passer to receiver pair, weighted by its passes and as wide as that weight (`edgeWidth` px for the heaviest pair, the others in proportion), with an arrow at the receiver. A player whose headshot resolves is drawn as it (`background-image`, the name below); any other is a node in the team's colour with the name's initials, with one warning naming them (soccer has no headshots, so every soccer player is this node; `headshots: false` asks for none). With `x`, `y`, `xend` and `yend`, each player sits at the mean of where they passed from and received, for the `preset` layout. `teamStylesheet(o)` is the stylesheet: nodes and edges whose `data.team` is a team take its colour; with `background`, every team colour keeps 3:1 contrast and aims to stay CIE76 ΔE 20 apart through the shared team palette (keeping the readable colour with one warning when it cannot; the `fallback` is drawn as given). A pair whose summed `weight` is 0 or less is 0 px wide. Sizes are px (Cytoscape style units), and fractions are refused. It never imports cytoscape; `cytoscape` (>= 3.34.3) is a new optional peer.

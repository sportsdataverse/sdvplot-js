New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvtables/mui` for MUI X Data Grid 9 (`@mui/x-data-grid`, the MIT community package only). `dataGridProps(spec, rows, o)` returns a `<DataGrid>`'s `columns`, `getRowId`, `getRowClassName`, `sx`, `density` and `rowHeight` from an sdvtables spec: each column's label, alignment and px width, sdvtables' number formats (`num`, `int`, `pct`, `rank`) through `valueFormatter` with numeric sorting, logo, wordmark and headshot cells through `renderCell` (abbreviations, names or numeric ids), `teamColorBg` / `teamColorBar` cells and whole team-coloured rows (`teamRows`) in sdvplot's team palette for the theme's background with black or white text by contrast, and the theme's colours, fonts, header style, stripes and density as MUI's CSS variables and `sx`, dark themes such as `midnight` included. A team or player with no image keeps its text and warns once; kinds drawn in HTML or SVG show their plain value, with one warning per kind. It never imports MUI and server-renders; `@mui/x-data-grid` >= 9.15.0 is an optional peer.

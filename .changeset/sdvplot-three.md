New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/three`: plain mesh buffers in world units for Three.js, with no three.js import. `surfaceMesh(sport, league)` is a sporty surface as one triangle mesh in the z = 0 plane (linear-sRGB vertex colours; a feature drawn as two loops in one path, or crossing itself at a corner, is split into simple loops before it is filled), `trajectoryTube(points, { radius, team, league })` a tube along a 3D path in a team's colour (radius in world units; a px `width`/`size` is refused), `teamColorPalette` the team colours (with `background`, 3:1 contrast and a CIE76 ΔE 20 target apart through the shared team palette, keeping the readable colour with one warning when it cannot), and `statcastPitchPath(pitch)` a Baseball Savant pitch's flight from release to home plate (Statcast's constant-acceleration fit; front-of-plate through 2025, middle-of-plate from 2026, by `game_year`). `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/react` gains `<Surface3D>` and `<Trajectory>` for react-three-fiber, which load `three` and `@react-three/fiber` on first render. `three` (>= 0.186.1) and `@react-three/fiber` (>= 9.8.1) are new optional peers.

Plot-native marks: sdvplot's Observable Plot marks behave like Plot's own.

- **Open image marks.** `logos`, `wordmarks` and `headshots` compute only the image URL, its size and skip-and-warn; every other Plot image option passes through (`tip`, `href`/`target`, `title`, `fx`/`fy`, `sort`/`filter`/`reverse`, `dx`/`dy`, `className`, `clip`, `opacity`, `channels`). `x` and `y` are Plot channels, so column names label the axes as on any Plot mark (pass `label: null` to hide them). Plot's row-preserving transforms wrap them (`dodgeY`/`dodgeX` beeswarms, `stackY`, `windowY`, `selectLast`, `pointer`); a transform that makes new rows (`bin`, `group`, `hexbin`) throws `InputError`, here and on the shot marks. A caller's `render`, `transform` and `initializer` are composed with sdvplot's, never replaced. `r` is the dodge collision radius (it never clips or sizes an image), and `alpha` is the Python sdvplot name for a constant `opacity` (passing both throws). Option types `ImageMarkOptions` and `ImagePassThrough`.
- **Tips, opt-in.** `tip: true` adds Plot's own tip, and the pointed row is the figure's `value` (with an `input` event). Default tip channels: the team on the image marks (or the player id on headshots); attempts, FG%, league FG% and the shrunk difference on `shotCells`; the zone, plus makes/attempts and FG% given `stats`, on `shotZones`; distance, FG%, league FG% and share on `shootingSignature`. A tip object's `format` merges over sdvplot's key by key.
- **Accessible names.** Every image is named by its subject ("KC logo", "KC wordmark", "3139477 headshot") in the Plot marks, `axisLogos`, the Vega image layers and d3's `appendLogos`/`appendWordmarks`/`appendHeadshots` (`ariaLabel` option). `shotZones` names each zone, `shotCells` each cell ("147 of 181 made, 81.2%"), and a titled `teamTiers` figure is labelled by its title. sdvplot's `surface` and d3 `appendSurface` take an `ariaDescription`. The README says where Plotly, ECharts and Chart.js charts get their accessible name.
- **Shot marks.** `shotCells` and `shotZones` draw the caller's `cells` and `areas` as the mark's data and take Plot's geo options (`GeoPassThrough`). `shotCells`' `dropOutside` drops cells centred outside the frame, leaving `clip` to Plot. `ShotCellsOptions.stroke`/`strokeWidth` and `ShotZonesOptions.fillOpacity` take Plot channels.
- **Pass-through on `axisLogos`, `meanLines`/`medianLines` and `teamTiers`.** `axisLogos` takes Plot's axis options (`AxisPassThrough`), the reference lines take Plot's rule options (`RuleLinePassThrough`, so `fx`/`fy` give a mean per facet), and `teamTiers` takes `tip`.
- `drawnMarks` (`sdvplot/testing`) reports a Plot image's x and y as the data value behind the channel: `null` where a transform such as `dodgeY` computed the position, and a Date as its time in ms.

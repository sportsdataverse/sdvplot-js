`sdvplot/chartjs`: Chart.js 4 team logo, wordmark and headshot point styles, axis-tick logos, faint logo watermarks and team colours; `chart.js` is an optional peer (>= 4.4).

`sdvplot/chartjs` `surface(league, o)`: a team-painted sporty court, field or rink as a Chart.js background plugin, with the linear scales that span it, for shot charts and hexbins (needs the optional peer `@sportsdataverse/sporty`, as `sdvplot/d3` does).

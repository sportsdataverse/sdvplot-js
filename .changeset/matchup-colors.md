`matchupColors(teamA, teamB, { league, theme })` (and `matchupColorsSync`): a two-team colour pair per theme, CIEDE2000 at least 20 apart and WCAG 2.5:1 on each theme's background; a port of Game on Paper's `pickGameColors`, matching it exactly on 69 real college-football matchups.

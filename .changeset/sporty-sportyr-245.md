Surfaces follow sportyR 2.4.5 (re-vendored at e021040, every oracle fixture regenerated). A feature with no area and no visible outline is no longer drawn, as in sportyR 2.4.1: zero-thickness lines such as the NLL sidelines and end lines, the NCAA men's goal arc and the NCAA men's neutral zone are left out (NBA draws 104 features, was 106), and the all-zero `custom` baseball, hockey and softball surfaces have no features. Hockey and lacrosse features are simple closed rings: the goaltender's trapezoid is two strips, the second set of faceoff lines mirrors the first, the centre faceoff circle and the benches close on their first point, the nhl98 crease's inner arc meets its inner sides, the nhl92 crease is its arc band and two L-marks, the lacrosse goal frame and net close, and the lacrosse goal mouth's stubs end on the inner arc. Rectangular lacrosse fields stop their defensive and offensive zones at the neutral zone. The contrasting lacrosse centre face-off marker compares hex colours by value, so a `center_line` of `#FFFFFF` or `#ffffffff` still gets it. Circles are drawn with `cospi`/`sinpi`, so quarter points sit exactly on the axes.

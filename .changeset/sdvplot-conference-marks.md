A conference, league or division name draws its mark: `logoUrl("PAC-12", "mbb")`, `logoUrl("Atlantic Division", "nhl")`, `logoUrl("American League", "mlb")` and every logo adapter read a value no team answers to as a conference or league row's key (or one of its `conference` aliases, such as "Southeastern Conference"), after every team alias, so cfb's "MAC" stays Macalester. A conference whose only official mark is a wordmark (the NHL's Conference Final marks) draws it as its logo. `suggest()` no longer offers conference keys or competition slugs, as sdvplot does.

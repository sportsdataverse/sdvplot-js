# @sportsdataverse/sdvplot

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 4fb32a6: New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/cytoscape`: passing networks for Cytoscape.js, as plain elements and stylesheet JSON. `passNetwork(rows, o)` returns `{ elements, style }`: one node per player and one directed edge per passer to receiver pair, weighted by its passes and as wide as that weight (`edgeWidth` px for the heaviest pair, the others in proportion), with an arrow at the receiver. A player whose headshot resolves is drawn as it (`background-image`, the name below); any other is a node in the team's colour with the name's initials, with one warning naming them (soccer has no headshots, so every soccer player is this node; `headshots: false` asks for none). With `x`, `y`, `xend` and `yend`, each player sits at the mean of where they passed from and received, for the `preset` layout. `teamStylesheet(o)` is the stylesheet: nodes and edges whose `data.team` is a team take its colour; with `background`, every team colour keeps 3:1 contrast and aims to stay CIE76 ΔE 20 apart through the shared team palette (keeping the readable colour with one warning when it cannot; the `fallback` is drawn as given). A pair whose summed `weight` is 0 or less is 0 px wide. Sizes are px (Cytoscape style units), and fractions are refused. It never imports cytoscape; `cytoscape` (>= 3.34.3) is a new optional peer.
- 972a189: New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/deckgl`: deck.gl layer props. `logoIconLayer` draws team logos at points (`IconLayer` props with auto-packed logo icons, centred, sized in px; a team with no logo is left out with one warning), `passArcLayer` draws arcs in team colours (`ArcLayer` props: the primary colour at the source, the secondary at the target; with `background`, every team colour keeps 3:1 contrast and aims to stay CIE76 ΔE 20 apart through the shared team palette, keeping the readable colour with one warning when it cannot, and an unresolved team takes `fallback` as given; `tilt: 90` lays the arcs flat for a top-down view and `height` sets how far they swing), and `surfaceLayer(sport, league)` draws a sporty surface as `PolygonLayer` props in its own `"cartesian"` coordinates. It never imports deck.gl; `@deck.gl/core` and `@deck.gl/layers` (>= 9.4.0) are new optional peers. The Python package's `sdvplot.pydeck` builds the same props, held to one shared fixture (`fixtures/deckgl/shared.json`).
- 4e61dac: New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/lightweight-charts` for TradingView Lightweight Charts (a new optional peer, >= 5.2.1; never imported): `withAttribution` returns `createChart` options with TradingView's attribution logo on (the link its Apache-2.0 licence asks for; only the explicit `{ attributedElsewhere: true }` turns it off), and `attributionNotice(container)` puts the licence's attribution notice (`TRADINGVIEW_NOTICE`, verbatim) on the page, once; `teamSeriesOptions` gives a line series its team's colour (with `background`, 3:1 contrast, and CIE76 ΔE 20 from the other teams on the chart as a target: the readable colour and one warning when it cannot) and reads its prices as probabilities ("58.5%") or American odds ("+150", rounded half away from zero, "0" never signed) on the price scale, crosshair and last-value line; `logoMarkers` is a series primitive painting team logos at points (a fraction of the pane tall; a team with no logo, or an image that fails to load, draws nothing and warns once; a probability that is not a fraction, or American odds under 100, are rejected). `drawnMarks` reads back what it drew.
- 70772c9: New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/nivo`: `teamColors`, a nivo `colors` accessor that draws each Bump series, Chord arc or SwarmPlot node in its team's colour (with `background` and `teams`, every colour keeps 3:1 contrast and aims to stay distinct from the others, CIE76 ΔE 20 or more, for dark mode; when no candidate is both, it keeps the readable colour and warns once); `logoLayer`, a custom layer of team logos at a Bump's line ends (or every point), a SwarmPlot's nodes or outside a Chord's arcs, sized as a fraction of the plot height; and `logoNode`, logos as a SwarmPlot's `circleComponent` or a Bump's `pointComponent`; plus `embedSources`. It never imports nivo: `@nivo/core`, `@nivo/bump`, `@nivo/swarmplot` and `@nivo/chord` are new optional peers (>= 0.99). `sdvplot/react` adds `<LogoBump>`, a nivo bump chart with team-coloured lines and a logo at the end of each line, which loads `@nivo/bump` only when it first renders.
- 7b2bd2e: New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/recharts`: Recharts render props that draw team logos and player headshots as a series' `dot` / `shape` (`logoDot`, `headshotDot`, with `last` for an end-dot), team logos as an axis' `tick` (`logoTick`, unresolved categories keep their text), and `teamColorPalette` (with `background`, every colour keeps 3:1 contrast and aims to stay distinct from the others, CIE76 ΔE 20 or more, for dark mode; when no candidate is both, it keeps the readable colour and warns once), plus `embedSources`. It never imports recharts, a new optional peer (>= 3.8, the oldest tested; 3.2 to 3.7 break server rendering or draw a reversed axis mirrored). `sdvplot/react` adds `<SurfaceBackdrop>`, a team-painted court, field or rink under a Recharts chart, which loads recharts and `@sportsdataverse/sporty` only when it first renders.
- 4471bb5: New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/svelte`, the first Svelte layer: Svelte 5 components for LayerChart charts, each a child of the chart's `<Svg>` layer. `LogoMark` draws team logos (or wordmarks) and `HeadshotMark` ESPN headshots at the rows' scaled (x, y), sized as a fraction of the plot area's height (a team or player with no image draws nothing and warns once); `SurfaceBackdrop` paints a team-coloured court, field or rink under the marks in the chart's x/y units; `teamColorPalette` gives one colour per team for the series, keeping 3:1 contrast against a `background` for dark mode and every team distinct where it can (a CIE76 ΔE of 20 or more is the target; when no candidate is both readable and distinct, it keeps the readable colour and warns once). The components ship as `.svelte` source under the `svelte` export condition (built with `@sveltejs/package`) and load layerchart, a new optional peer (>= 2.5.1, the release it is tested against, with `svelte` >= 5.57, the oldest that compiles it), only when they render: importing `./svelte` never needs it, and a missing peer throws an install message from the component that needs it.
- f6731f1: New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/three`: plain mesh buffers in world units for Three.js, with no three.js import. `surfaceMesh(sport, league)` is a sporty surface as one triangle mesh in the z = 0 plane (linear-sRGB vertex colours; a feature drawn as two loops in one path, or crossing itself at a corner, is split into simple loops before it is filled), `trajectoryTube(points, { radius, team, league })` a tube along a 3D path in a team's colour (radius in world units; a px `width`/`size` is refused), `teamColorPalette` the team colours (with `background`, 3:1 contrast and a CIE76 ΔE 20 target apart through the shared team palette, keeping the readable colour with one warning when it cannot), and `statcastPitchPath(pitch)` a Baseball Savant pitch's flight from release to home plate (Statcast's constant-acceleration fit; front-of-plate through 2025, middle-of-plate from 2026, by `game_year`). `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/react` gains `<Surface3D>` and `<Trajectory>` for react-three-fiber, which load `three` and `@react-three/fiber` on first render. `three` (>= 0.186.1) and `@react-three/fiber` (>= 9.8.1) are new optional peers.
- 4f1f2e0: New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/uplot`: `withTeamSeries` strokes (and with `fill`, fills) each uPlot series in its team's colour, keeping 3:1 contrast against a `background` for dark mode and every team distinct where it can (a CIE76 ΔE of 20 or more is the target; when no candidate is both readable and distinct, it keeps the readable colour and warns once), and `logoPlugin` is a uPlot plugin that paints team logos on the canvas at data points in its `draw` hook (sized as a fraction of the plot area; a team with no logo, or an image that fails to load, draws nothing and warns once; rows can be a function of the chart, so end logos follow live `setData`). `drawnMarks` reads back what the plugin drew. It never imports uplot, a new optional peer (>= 1.6.31).
- 7df7161: New subpath `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/victory`: `teamTheme`, a Victory theme from team colours (the teams' colours as every group, stack, pie and legend `colorScale`, the first team's for a lone line, area, bar or scatter, built over a `base` theme such as `VictoryTheme.clean`), and `teamColorPalette`, the same colours as a `colorScale` list (with `background`, every colour keeps 3:1 contrast and aims to stay distinct from the others, CIE76 ΔE 20 or more, for dark mode; when no candidate is both, it keeps the readable colour and warns once); plus `embedSources`. It never imports victory: `victory` is a new optional peer (>= 37.3.6). `sdvplot/react` adds `<LogoPoint>`, team logos as a `VictoryScatter`'s points (each as tall as the point it replaces), and `<LogoTick>`, team logos as a `VictoryAxis`' tick labels (`size` px tall); a label with no logo keeps its text, drawn by Victory's `VictoryLabel`, which `<LogoTick>` loads from victory only then.

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 0b91a00: Team identity for every SportsDataverse league: `resolve`/`suggest` (100% parity with the Python sdvplot package on 8,403 real inputs), team colours and palettes, logo, wordmark and headshot URLs, and the React primitives `TeamLogo`, `Wordmark` and `Headshot`. League data loads lazily per league; the 3.4 MB nflverse gsis-id map loads only through `loadGsis()` (or `preloadAll()`). Warnings go through one once-per-key channel (`warn`, `setWarningHandler`, `resetWarnings`), which `@sportsdataverse/sdvtables` shares.
- 0b91a00: Observable Plot and d3 integrations (`sdvplot/plot`, `sdvplot/d3`; both import the optional peer `@sportsdataverse/sporty`, so install it with them); the adapter-contract suite (`sdvplot/testing`); the lazily fetched full logo manifest (`marks({ full: true })`); `rowsFrom` (column form to rows) and the React `useResolve` hook.
- c27916f: `sdvplot/chartjs`: Chart.js 4 team logo, wordmark and headshot point styles, axis-tick logos, faint logo watermarks and team colours; `chart.js` is an optional peer (>= 4.4). A `loadImage` option on every image helper (`logoPoints`, `wordmarkPoints`, `headshotPoints`, `axisLogos`, `logoWatermarks`) loads the marks through your own loader instead of a DOM image, so charts render in Node on `@napi-rs/canvas` (pass its `loadImage`); images that arrive after a draw redraw the chart. Type `ImageLike`.

  `sdvplot/chartjs/surface` `surface(league, o)`: a team-painted sporty court, field or rink as a Chart.js background plugin, with the linear scales that span it, for shot charts and hexbins (it imports the optional peer `@sportsdataverse/sporty`, so install sporty to use it; `sdvplot/chartjs` itself does not need sporty).

- 854acbe: `matchupColors(teamA, teamB, { league, theme })` (and `matchupColorsSync`): a two-team colour pair per theme, CIEDE2000 at least 20 apart and WCAG 2.5:1 on each theme's background; a port of Game on Paper's `pickGameColors`, matching it exactly on 69 real college-football matchups.
- fa3ce31: `sdvplot/export` (Node): `toPNG(svg, { width, scale, background, color, images })` rasterizes an SVG figure with the optional peer `@resvg/resvg-js`, downloading its remote logos and headshots (`DownloadError` on a failed download; `images: "skip"` leaves them out). `socialCard(svg, { aspect, padding, background, gravity, color })` frames it on a fixed-ratio canvas as `gt_social_crop` does. Helpers: `svgSize`, `parseAspect`, `parseGravity`, `checkColor`, `canvasFor`, `offsetFor`, `peerMissing`. A missing optional peer throws `OptionalDependencyError`.
- 06ef977: Linked interactivity: brush, hover and select across figures and tables that share one selection store.

  - **Store (core).** `createSelection()` holds `hover`, `selected`, `predicate` (a brush region as a row test) and `cursor` (one shared hover value, `{ field, value }`). It notifies once per change and not at all for a change that changes nothing, and its snapshots are read-only at runtime. Helpers `focusIds`, `toId`, `sameIds`, `sameCursor`; types `Cursor`, `RowFilter`, `SelectionState`, `SelectionPatch`, `SelectionStore`.
  - **`sdvplot/interact`** (optional peer `d3`): `brushFilter(figure, store, options)` returns a `BrushFilterHandle` (a rectangle, or a 1-D brush along `x` or `y`; a brush that holds no row dims every mark, or filters nothing with `empty: "clear"`; `scales` brushes a d3-drawn `<svg>`; a row on the region's edge counts as inside; `move(region)` brushes in data coordinates); `linkSelection(store, { figure, table, hover, select })` (`LinkSelectionOptions`) links one Plot figure or one sdvtables table per call, with `select: "toggle"` turning each linked mark into an accessible checkbox; `linkCursor` draws the shared cursor through each figure's own scale as a rule, a band or a ring; `nearestHover` and `tooltip` serve d3 and other non-Plot figures; `highlight` and `hasDom`. Without a DOM every call is a no-op, so server-rendered markup is unchanged; a bad option still throws `InputError`.
  - **Link ids.** `logos`, `wordmarks` and `headshots` take an `id` option, and `linkIds(rows, id)` is a `render` that stamps any other Plot mark: each mark carries `data-sdv-id` (by default the resolved ESPN team id, or the player id on headshots), which follows its row through `sort`, `filter`, facets and `Plot.dodgeY`. `sizeRender` takes the link ids as an optional fourth argument.
  - **React.** `useSelection(store)` in `sdvplot/react` re-renders on every store change.
  - **Teardown.** Clearing a brush clears only that brush's region, so a selection made elsewhere survives. Tearing a link down restores the figure or table it changed, and the store keeps its state for the views still linked. A figure takes one `select: "toggle"` link; a second, or one on marks inside an `<a href>`, throws `InputError`.
  - **Styling** is a class toggle (`.sdv-focus` on the figure, `.sdv-hl` on the emphasised marks), never a redraw, through CSS custom properties: `--sdv-dim-opacity` (0.2), `--sdv-cursor-color` (`rgba(0,0,0,.2)`), `--sdv-cursor-width` (`10px`), `--sdv-tip-bg` (`rgba(34,34,34,.85)`) and `--sdv-tip-fg` (`#ddd`).

- 85a7fab: Plot-native marks: sdvplot's Observable Plot marks behave like Plot's own.

  - **Open image marks.** `logos`, `wordmarks` and `headshots` compute only the image URL, its size and skip-and-warn; every other Plot image option passes through (`tip`, `href`/`target`, `title`, `fx`/`fy`, `sort`/`filter`/`reverse`, `dx`/`dy`, `className`, `clip`, `opacity`, `channels`). `x` and `y` are Plot channels, so column names label the axes as on any Plot mark (pass `label: null` to hide them). Plot's row-preserving transforms wrap them (`dodgeY`/`dodgeX` beeswarms, `stackY`, `windowY`, `selectLast`, `pointer`); a transform that makes new rows (`bin`, `group`, `hexbin`) throws `InputError`, here and on the shot marks. A caller's `render`, `transform` and `initializer` are composed with sdvplot's, never replaced. `r` is the dodge collision radius (it never clips or sizes an image), and `alpha` is the Python sdvplot name for a constant `opacity` (passing both throws). Option types `ImageMarkOptions` and `ImagePassThrough`.
  - **Tips, opt-in.** `tip: true` adds Plot's own tip, and the pointed row is the figure's `value` (with an `input` event). Default tip channels: the team on the image marks (or the player id on headshots); attempts, FG%, league FG% and the shrunk difference on `shotCells`; the zone, plus makes/attempts and FG% given `stats`, on `shotZones`; distance, FG%, league FG% and share on `shootingSignature`. A tip object's `format` merges over sdvplot's key by key.
  - **Accessible names.** Every image is named by its subject ("KC logo", "KC wordmark", "3139477 headshot") in the Plot marks, `axisLogos`, the Vega image layers and d3's `appendLogos`/`appendWordmarks`/`appendHeadshots` (`ariaLabel` option). `shotZones` names each zone, `shotCells` each cell ("147 of 181 made, 81.2%"), and a titled `teamTiers` figure is labelled by its title. sdvplot's `surface` and d3 `appendSurface` take an `ariaDescription`. The README says where Plotly, ECharts and Chart.js charts get their accessible name.
  - **Shot marks.** `shotCells` and `shotZones` draw the caller's `cells` and `areas` as the mark's data and take Plot's geo options (`GeoPassThrough`). `shotCells`' `dropOutside` drops cells centred outside the frame, leaving `clip` to Plot. `ShotCellsOptions.stroke`/`strokeWidth` and `ShotZonesOptions.fillOpacity` take Plot channels.
  - **Pass-through on `axisLogos`, `meanLines`/`medianLines` and `teamTiers`.** `axisLogos` takes Plot's axis options (`AxisPassThrough`), the reference lines take Plot's rule options (`RuleLinePassThrough`, so `fx`/`fy` give a mean per facet), and `teamTiers` takes `tip`.
  - `drawnMarks` (`sdvplot/testing`) reports a Plot image's x and y as the data value behind the channel: `null` where a transform such as `dodgeY` computed the position, and a Date as its time in ms.

- 991106f: Shot-chart utilities, ported from the Blazing the Nets shot charts, with hexagon or square cells:

  - `sdvplot/bins`, dependency-free x/y binning for any data: `hexbin` and `hexagonPath` (a d3-hexbin port), `squarebin` and `squarePath`, `binner` (hexagons, squares, or squares of a hexagon's area from one options object), `cellPath`, `cellPoints`.
  - `sdvplot/shots` (re-exports the binners; install `@sportsdataverse/sporty` with it): `binShots`, `leagueIndex`, `cellsVsLeague`, `cellsVsDistance`, `shrunkDiff`, `LEAGUE_PRIOR_ATTEMPTS`, `sizeCells`, `statsByZone`, `fgPctByDistance`, `vsLeague`, `statsBySide`, `signaturePoints`, `diffScale`.
  - Plot marks `shotCells` (hexagons or squares via `shape`), `shotZones`, `shootingSignature`.
  - `shotZones`' labels take no pointer events, so a click or hover on a label reaches its zone; with `text`, each zone's accessible name carries its label's text ("Paint (non-RA), 136/326").
  - d3 `appendLegend` (diff colour bar plus a hexagon or square size key) and `appendSignature`; `appendHeadshots` draws circular faces (`clip: "circle"`, `ring`, `placeholder`).
  - React `<Headshot fallback="initials" name="…">`: the player's initials when there is no headshot or it fails to load, server-rendered pages included. The initials box reads the `--sdv-line` CSS variable.

- 7bd55a6: `toPNG(svg, { fonts: { files, defaultFamily, system } })`: the fonts resvg draws `<text>` with. On a host without system fonts (slim Docker images, minimal CI runners) resvg drew every label as nothing, with no error; `fonts.files` loads font files from disk (a missing file throws `InputError`), `defaultFamily` names the fallback family, and `system: false` skips the system fonts. The default is unchanged: the system fonts.
- fb1088b: `sdvplot/plotly`, `sdvplot/vega`, `sdvplot/echarts`: spec adapters with no runtime dependency (`withLogos`, `withWordmarks`, `withHeadshots`, `withAxisLogos`, team colour helpers, `embedSources`). They return a new spec and never mutate yours (`checkAdapterContract` in `sdvplot/testing` checks this for any adapter).

  - The images they add stay out of your tooltips: ECharts helper series set `tooltip: { show: false }`, so an axis tooltip lists only your own series; Vega image layers set `tooltip: null`, which outranks `config.mark.tooltip` and a parent `encoding.tooltip`.
  - Each Vega image layer carries a description ("KC logo") rather than reading out its URL or data URI.
  - Plotly `withAxisLogos` hides the drawn tick labels with `showticklabels: false`, so hovering a bar still names its team; an unresolved team keeps its text tick.

Five charts of Brooklyn's first 2000 shots of 2025-26 (see
[Sample data](/sample-data#bkn_shots_2026-and-nba_league_2026-a-teams-shots-and-the-league)) share one selection
store, and the store holds one number: the shot distance under the pointer. The design is blazing-the-nets' `master`
dashboard (`src/containers/Charts/index.js`), where six charts follow one `hover.distance`, rebuilt here with
`@sportsdataverse/sdvplot`'s shot kit and its `interact` functions.

Hover the share bars, the FG% bars or the side chart. `linkCursor` draws the distance through each chart's own scale:
a 1 ft band on the share bars, the 3 ft band that holds it on the FG% bars, a band up the side chart, a rule on the
shooting signature, and a ring around the hoop on the court. A move inside one foot writes nothing to the store, and
leaving a chart clears every cursor at once. On the court, Plot's tip finds the nearest cell within 18 px;
`linkSelection` lights that cell and dims the rest, and the cursor stays where it was. The menu above the court
switches between hexagons and squares of the same area.

Each part on its own: [shot charts](../guides/shot-charts.mdx) draws the cells, zones and the signature, and
[blazing-the-nets](../guides/blazing-the-nets.mdx) maps the site's charts onto sdvplot.

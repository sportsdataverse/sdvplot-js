[reactablefmtr](https://kcuilla.github.io/reactablefmtr/) gives R's reactable tables data bars, icon sets, bubbles and
colour tiles, and draws sparklines, sparkbars and gauges in cells. sdvtables has the same formatters as column kinds and options, with reactablefmtr's defaults, and adds
team awareness: where reactablefmtr reads a colour column (`fill_color_ref`, `color_ref`), sdvtables also takes a team
column and a league and paints each row in its team's colour, with the text kept readable. Everything is inline HTML
and SVG, so a table needs no icon font, tooltip library or script. Every example uses the eight 2024 AFC teams of the
[sample data](/sample-data); the gallery is searchable by the reactablefmtr name (`data_bars`, `icon_sets`, ...).

## Data bars

`dataBar` (reactablefmtr `data_bars`) draws a bar whose length is the value over the column's largest absolute value,
or the value scaled between `minValue` and `maxValue`. One `fill` colour paints every bar and several ramp over the
column's range (`bias` as in grDevices::colorRamp); `gradient` paints each bar left to right instead. `colorRef` reads a
colour column, or `team` and `league` use each team's colour, and `mark: "logo"` (or `"wordmark"`, or `"headshot"`
with `markKey`) puts the team's mark at the end of the bar. `textPosition` is one of `inside-end` (the default),
`inside-base`, `center`, `outside-end`, `outside-base`, `above` and `none`; `align: "right"` grows the bars from the
right, and `roundEdges`, `barHeight` and `numberFormat` finish them.

## Diverging data bars

A column with a negative value (or `posNeg: true`) draws its bars either side of a centre line. Given two fill colours,
the first paints the negatives and the second the positives. A missing value stays blank.

<Live id="sdvtables/formatters/dataBar-diverging" />

## Icon sets

`iconSet` (reactablefmtr `icon_sets`) puts an icon beside each value. The icons and the colours are each dealt out in
equal-count buckets of the column (dplyr::ntile), so with three colours the bottom third takes the first. The icon goes
to the `right` (the default), `left`, `above`, `below` or `over` the value.

<Live id="sdvtables/kinds/iconSet" />

## Icons per row

`iconRef` and `iconColorRef` read each row's icon and colour from columns instead of the buckets. `ICON_NAMES` (from
`@sportsdataverse/sdvtables/html`) lists every icon; they are plain SVG paths drawn for sdvtables, not Font Awesome.

<Live id="sdvtables/formatters/icon-names" />

## Icon assign

`iconAssign` (reactablefmtr `icon_assign`) writes a value as a row of icons: one per unit up to the column's maximum,
filled up to the value, or with `buckets` the value's bucket out of that many. `showValues` adds the number to the
`left`, `right`, `above` or `below`.

<Live id="sdvtables/kinds/iconAssign" />

## Merged columns

`mergeStack` (reactablefmtr `merge_column`) shows a second column's value beside the cell's own (`position`: `right`,
`left`, `above` or `below`), each with its own size, colour, weight and style. Like a data bar, it can lead with a
logo, wordmark or headshot. `mergeStackTeamColor` remains for a lower line in the team's colour.

<Live id="sdvtables/kinds/mergeStack" />

## Bubbles

`bubble` (reactablefmtr `bubble_grid`) draws a circle, or with `shape: "squares"` a square, whose width is the value
over the column's largest, coloured on a ramp of the column's range or by `colorRef` / `team`. reactablefmtr's largest
bubble is 100 pixels; `maxSize` changes it.

<Live id="sdvtables/kinds/bubble" />

## Colour tiles and scales

`colorRanks` fills the cell, as reactablefmtr's `color_scales` and `color_tiles` do, and takes their options. `span`
puts several columns on one scale (`true`: every numeric column), `bias` moves the palette's stops, `evenBreaks` deals
the palette out in equal-count buckets instead of a ramp, `showText: false` keeps only the colour, and `colorRef` or
`team` gives a row its own fill.

<Live id="sdvtables/formatters/colorRanks-tiles" />

## Pill buttons in team colours

`colorPills` with `colorRef`, or `team` and `league`, colours each pill by its row (reactablefmtr `pill_buttons` with
`color_ref`); the text turns black or white to stay readable.

<Live id="sdvtables/formatters/colorPills-team" />

## Highlighting the minimum and maximum

`highlight` takes `"min"`, `"max"` or `"both"` in place of a predicate (reactablefmtr `highlight_min`,
`highlight_max` and `highlight_min_max`): the extremes, every tie included, turn bold, the minimum red and the maximum
green. `textColor` and `fill` change the colours.

<Live id="sdvtables/formatters/highlight-extremes" />

## Tooltips

`tooltip: true` on any column writes the cell's value as its `title` attribute, which the browser shows on hover. It
needs no tooltip library; a cell that shows only an image takes the raw value.

<Live id="sdvtables/formatters/tooltip" />

## In-cell charts

reactablefmtr draws sparklines and sparkbars with the dataui htmlwidget and gauges with nested `div`s. sdvtables draws
all three as an inline `<svg role="img">` per cell, with no script: each has an `aria-label` and a `<title>` (the
browser's tooltip) summarising its values, the minimum, maximum and last for a series, plus the reference line and band
when drawn. `sparkline` and `sparkbar` read an array column, one array of numbers per row; a `null`, `NaN` or infinite
entry is a gap and a missing or empty array leaves the cell blank. Like the other formatters, `team` and `league` (or
`colorRef`) colour each row; a team colour is checked for 3:1 contrast against the table's background and gives way to
the team's other colour, or a blend of it, when it would not show. The weekly series come from
[`AFC_WEEKS_2024`](/sample-data).

### Sparklines

`sparkline` (reactablefmtr `react_sparkline`) joins the points with straight segments; a gap breaks the line and a
point with gaps on both sides draws as a dot. `highlightPoints` marks the `first`, `last`, `min` or `max` point (or
`all`): a list of names draws them red, or give a colour per name, as reactablefmtr's `highlight_points(min = "red")`.
`statline` draws a dashed line at the series' `mean`, `median`, `min` or `max`, labelled at its right end. The chart is
`chartWidth` by `chartHeight` pixels (100 by 22); columns are not sortable by default.

<Live id="sdvtables/kinds/sparkline" />

### Areas, bands and labels

`showArea` fills under each unbroken run of points, `bandline` shades the `innerquartiles` (R's type 7 quantiles, as
dataui) or the whole `range`, and `labels` prints the values of the named points in the cell's text colour: `first` and
`last` alone sit beside their points, any other set above them, and the default 22-pixel height grows to make room as
in reactablefmtr. `minValue` and `maxValue` put every row on one scale; `build()` rejects a `minValue` that is not
below `maxValue`.

<Live id="sdvtables/formatters/sparkline-bands" />

### Sparkbars

`sparkbar` (reactablefmtr `react_sparkbar`) draws a bar per value standing on zero, so a negative value hangs below
it and the smallest positive bar still shows (dataui's did not). It takes the sparkline's `statline`, `bandline`,
`labels` and bounds, and `highlightBars` recolours bars the way `highlightPoints` marks points.

<Live id="sdvtables/kinds/sparkbar" />

### Gauges

`gauge` (reactablefmtr `gauge_chart`) fills a half circle from `minValue` (0) to `maxValue` (by default the column's
largest absolute value). As in reactablefmtr the gauge fills by the absolute value, so -5 fills as far as 5; the printed
number keeps its sign and the `aria-label` says the absolute value was gauged. Several `fill` colours ramp over the
column's range (`bias` as in grDevices::colorRamp), `showMinMax` prints the scale's ends, `size: 2` doubles the
50-pixel gauge, and `numberFormat` prints the value. A missing or infinite value is blank and text passes through.

<Live id="sdvtables/kinds/gauge" />

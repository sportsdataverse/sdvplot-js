Every cell kind and decoration has its own page in the [gallery](/gallery/sdvtables/), one feature at a time. These
recipes put several together into finished tables, the way reactable and reactablefmtr showcases do: league marks,
colour scales, a theme and, where the table is long enough to need it, sorting, filtering and paging. Each one runs on
the [sample data](/sample-data).

## NBA standings, sortable and filterable

Logos, a team-colour bar beside each nickname and the wins coloured by rank, for the 2023-24 Pacific Division. Click a
header to sort or type a city into the filter box; games behind and win % are computed from the captured wins and
losses.

<Live id="sdvtables/recipes/nba-pacific" />

## NHL standings, grouped

The Atlantic Division split into the top three, who qualify for the playoffs, and everyone else. `groupBy` and
`groupStripes` mark the two groups, `tally` writes the record and `percentileBar` places each team's points percentage
on a track.

<Live id="sdvtables/recipes/nhl-atlantic" />

## NFL EPA per play, paged

All 32 teams, eight to a page, with logos and wordmarks. Offence and defence share one colour scale, so one legend
reads for both, and `delta` shows the net with an arrow.

<Live id="sdvtables/recipes/nfl-epa" />

## A game log in the team's colours

The `sdvTeam` theme takes its heading colour from the team named in its options. Here it frames Philadelphia's 2024
regular season: opponent logos, results, scores and margins, six games to a page.

<Live id="sdvtables/recipes/eagles-game-log" />

## Quarterbacks on a dark theme

`midnight` is a dark theme. The table shows ESPN headshots, each quarterback's name above his team in the team colour, and a
missing value shown as an empty pill. `rowAccent` turns the division into a coloured left edge and `spotlight` lights
Buffalo and dims the rest.

<Live id="sdvtables/recipes/afc-quarterbacks-dark" />

## A hockey scoring summary

The four goals of the PWHL's game 42, with team logos looked up by HockeyTech id, each scorer above the team name in
its colour, and the score after each goal. A `cutline` marks the end of each period but the last.

<Live id="sdvtables/recipes/pwhl-scoring" />

## World Cup passing

France's completed first-half passes in the 2018 final, one row per passer: mean length, metres gained toward goal as
colour pills, and the share that ended in the attacking third as a percentile bar. The table is sortable and pages six
passers at a time. Passers are shown by StatsBomb player id: the fixture carries no names.

<Live id="sdvtables/recipes/wc2018-passing" />

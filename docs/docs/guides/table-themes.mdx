A theme sets everything about a table that is not its data: fonts, colours, rules, spacing. sdvtables ships the 20
themes of sdvplotR and gtUtils and the 27 of reactablefmtr, each one function call on the builder, so a table can
match a site, a publication or a team without any CSS of your own. The
[theme gallery](/gallery/sdvtables/#every-theme) shows every one on the same rows.

## One theme

`.theme(name)` picks a theme by name. Most themes load their Google Fonts with the table.

## A team's own colours

`sdvTeam` fills the header with a team's primary colour: pass `{ league, team }` as its options. The title takes
`onColor` of the fill, and the subtitle `secondaryOn`, a blend of the title ink toward the fill that still reads at
4.5:1.

## reactablefmtr themes

The 27 themes of [reactablefmtr](https://kcuilla.github.io/reactablefmtr/) are registered as `fmtr-<name>`
(`fmtr-espn`, `fmtr-nytimes`, `fmtr-fivethirtyeight`, ...), so they never collide with the house themes: the house
`midnight` and reactablefmtr's `fmtr-midnight` are different tables. `no_lines` is `fmtr-no-lines`, and `GT_ALIASES`
maps each reactablefmtr name to its theme. Colours, sizes, rules and padding are reactablefmtr's own, read from its
source. A few were moved just far enough to pass sdvtables' 3:1 contrast checks on the row cues and the filter
boxes: the body type of espn, lux, minty, pff, spacelab and sunrise, and the stripe and hover fills of darkly,
hoverlight and midnight.

<Live id="sdvtables/themes/fmtr-espn" />

Each theme function's arguments are options: `fontSize`, `fontColor`, `headerFontSize`, `headerFontColor`,
`backgroundColor` and `cellPadding`. reactable's `striped` and `highlight` are options too (`"true"`), off by default as
in reactable. The highlight fills the row under the pointer (the engine's hovered row), and is on by default in
`fmtr-hoverdark` and `fmtr-hoverlight`.

<Live id="sdvtables/themes-api/fmtr-options" />

`fmtr-team` is reactablefmtr's `default` look with the header filled in a team's primary colour (`{ league, team }`),
the header ink picked by contrast, a rule in the secondary colour, and stripe and hover tints that keep the body type
at 4.5:1.

<Live id="sdvtables/themes/fmtr-team" />

`FMTR_THEME_NAMES` lists the 27; hand it to `themePreview` to see them side by side.

## The registry

`THEMES` maps each name in `THEME_NAMES` to the function that builds it, and `resolveTheme` picks and calls one.
A theme's tokens (`TOKEN_KEYS`) become CSS custom properties on the table.

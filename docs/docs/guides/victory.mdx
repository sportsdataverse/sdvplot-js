[Victory](https://commerce.nearform.com/open-source/victory/) builds charts from React components and lets you swap the
component that draws each point or label. sdvplot gives it four pieces. `teamTheme` and `teamColorPalette`, in
`@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/victory`, colour a chart by team. `<LogoPoint>` and `<LogoTick>`, in
`@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/react`, draw team logos as a scatter's points and as an axis' tick labels. The colour helpers
never import victory (install `victory`, 37.3.6 or later, beside them: an optional peer), and all four are
synchronous, so load the league first (`loadLeague`). Each chart below is drawn by Victory in your browser.

## Scoring margins, each opponent's logo at the end of its bar

The Chiefs' 2024 regular season, game by game: the bar is the scoring margin and the logo at its end is the opponent.
`<LogoPoint league team />` is the `dataComponent` of a `VictoryScatter`: Victory hands it each point's position and
data row, and it draws the logo of the row's `team` field there, in place of the point's symbol. `teamTheme({ league,
teams, base })` is the chart's theme. Built over Victory's own `VictoryTheme.clean`, whose axes and fonts it keeps, it
gives a lone bar series the first team's colour.

<Live id="sdvplot/victory/kc-margins" />

Victory hands a `dataComponent` pixels and no plot height, so the logo is sized in px: as tall as the point it
replaces, twice the scatter's `size`. Set `size` on the `VictoryScatter`. A `height` (the spec adapters' fraction of the
plot) throws. A team with no logo draws nothing and warns once, as the ECharts adapter does. Victory's event handlers
(tooltips, a voronoi container) stay on the logo. A `wordmark` `markType`, `alpha`, a `season` (a value or a field) and
`embed` (from `embedSources`) work as in the other adapters.

## Logo tick labels on a dark background

`<LogoTick league size />` is a `VictoryAxis`' `tickLabelComponent`: each tick label that names a team is drawn as the
team's logo, `size` px tall (default 24), on the side of Victory's label anchor the text would have run (under a bottom
axis, left of a left one). The logos stay upright: they do not rotate with a tick label's `angle`. A label with no
logo keeps its text, drawn by Victory's own `VictoryLabel`. `<LogoTick>`
loads that from `victory` when it first draws one, and warns once. A missing `victory` throws `OptionalDependencyError`
then. On a server render such a label is left out until the page's scripts run; the logos themselves render on the
server.

The bars take their colours from `teamColorPalette(league, teams, { background })`, one per team in order. On a dark
background a team colour under 3:1 contrast gives way to the team's other colour, or is lightened until it reads, and a
colour that would then sit too close to an earlier team's (CIE76 ΔE under 20) takes the team's other colour, or else
the colour nearest its own (its lightness, hue or chroma stepped) that is distinct and still reads; if none is, it
keeps the readable colour and warns once. Here Denver and
New England share one navy. The chart's own background and axis ink are yours to set (here in the axes'
`style`), or come with the base theme.

## Theme or list

`teamColorPalette` has the shape of the ECharts, Recharts and Svelte adapters' `teamColorPalette`, `(league, teams,
options)`, and returns the list Victory takes as a `colorScale`. `teamTheme` is the Victory-native form of the same
colours: it writes them into a theme as the `colorScale` of every group, stack, pie and legend, so each child of a
`VictoryGroup` is drawn in its team's colour without a `colorScale` prop, and gives the first team's colour to a lone
line, area, bar or scatter. Pass the teams in the order the chart draws them. Everything else comes from `base`; without
one, Victory draws the axes unstyled.

## React Native

These pieces target Victory on the web and have not been tried in React Native. `victory-native` 41 and later is a
different library, drawn with Skia, with no theme object and no `dataComponent`, so `teamTheme`, `<LogoPoint>` and
`<LogoTick>` (react-dom SVG) do not carry over. The colours `teamColorPalette` returns are plain hex strings, which a
victory-native series takes as its `color`, and `logoUrlSync` gives a logo's URL to load as a Skia image.

## Reading back what was drawn

Every image carries the `data-sdv-*` attributes that `drawnMarks` from `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/testing` reads, so a
test can render the chart (jsdom, or a browser) and check which teams were drawn where. `x` and `y` are Victory's pixel
positions: a point's centre, or a tick label's anchor. `height` reads in px, the size the logo was drawn at, because
Victory hands neither component a plot height.

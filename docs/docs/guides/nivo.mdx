[nivo](https://nivo.rocks) draws charts as React components, and lets you add layers of your own on top of a chart's
built-in ones, or swap the component that draws each node. `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/nivo` gives you three pieces for
it: `teamColors`, a chart's `colors`; `logoLayer`, a custom layer of team logos over a Bump, SwarmPlot or Chord; and
`logoNode`, logos as the nodes themselves. It never imports nivo (install `@nivo/core` and the chart's package, 0.99
or later, beside it: optional peers), and it is synchronous, so load the league first (`loadLeague`). The ready-made
bump chart is a component, `<LogoBump>`, in `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/react`. Each chart below is drawn by nivo in
your browser.

## A weekly power ranking, a logo at the end of each line

`teamColors({ league })` is a `colors` accessor: a Bump series (and a Chord arc) is read by its `id`, so a series whose
`id` is a team is drawn in the team's colour. `logoLayer({ league, height })` goes into the chart's `layers`, after the
built-in layers it should sit over. On a Bump it draws a logo at each series' last point (`at: "first"` or `"all"` for
the others; a week with no rank has no point), reading the team from the series' `id` or, with `team`, from a field of
the series object. The ranks here come from the AFC East's and West's 2024 results, week by week: most wins so far (a
tie counts half), then the best point differential, then the name. Denver and New England share one navy, and
Buffalo's is close, so the chart passes its `background` and every team (`teams`): each colour then reads and stays
apart from the others (New England moves off Denver's navy to a blue of its own), as in the dark-mode section below.

<Live id="sdvplot/nivo/afc-power-ranks" />

`height` is a fraction of the plot height, in (0, 1], as in the spec adapters: nivo hands a custom layer the plot
area's `innerHeight`, so a px value (24) throws. A team with no logo draws nothing and warns once, as the ECharts
adapter does. The logos sit at nivo's final positions: with `animate` on they appear at the end of the motion rather
than travel with it. The layer's group has `pointer-events: none`, so the lines under it keep their hover.

## `<LogoBump>` on a dark background

`<LogoBump league data />` is nivo's `Bump` with both of the above: team-coloured lines and a logo at the end of each
line (`logoHeight`, `at`). Every other prop goes to nivo as is. With `width` and `height` it draws at that size, without
them it fills its parent (`ResponsiveBump`). `background` turns on the dark-mode palette: a team colour under 3:1
contrast against it gives way to the team's other colour, or is lightened until it reads, and a colour that would then
sit too close to an earlier team's (CIE76 ΔE under 20) takes the team's other colour, or else the colour nearest its
own (its lightness, hue or chroma stepped) that is distinct and still reads; if none is, it keeps the readable colour
and warns once. `@nivo/bump` loads when the
component first renders, never with `sdvplot/react`, and a missing package throws `OptionalDependencyError` then. A
server render draws nothing; the chart appears once the page's scripts run. (nivo's own `Bump` with `logoLayer`
renders on the server, logos included.)

<Live id="sdvplot/nivo/logo-bump-dark" />

For the same palette on nivo's own chart, pass every team the chart draws: `teamColors({ league, teams, background
})`. The colours depend on the whole set, so `background` without `teams` throws, and a team outside `teams` is drawn
in the `fallback` colour, with one warning. `teamColors` is the nivo counterpart of `teamColorPalette` in the ECharts,
Recharts and Svelte adapters: an accessor nivo calls per datum, rather than a list of colours.

## Logos as the nodes

`logoNode({ league, team })` is a SwarmPlot's `circleComponent` (or a Bump's `pointComponent`): each node is drawn as
its team's logo, as tall as nivo sized the node (the chart's `size` or `pointSize`, in px), in place of the circle. The
team is the node's `id`, or with `team` a field of its datum. It does not wire up the mouse handlers nivo hands a node,
so pass `useMesh` for tooltips. The data is nflverse's 2024 regular season: EPA per play on offense and on defense.

## A Chord

On a Chord, `teamColors({ league })` colours each arc and its ribbons by the arc's id (the chart's `keys`), and
`logoLayer({ league, height })` in its `layers` puts each team's logo outside the ring at the middle of its
arc, `height` × the plot height tall. nivo sizes the ring to the plot area, so leave room for the logos in the chart's
`margin`.

## Reading back what was drawn

Every image carries the `data-sdv-*` attributes that `drawnMarks` from `@sportsdataverse/sdvplot/testing` reads, so a
test can render the chart (jsdom, or a browser) and check which teams were drawn where. `x` and `y` are nivo's pixel
positions inside the plot area. A `logoLayer` image's `height` reads as the fraction of the plot height it was given; a
`logoNode` image's reads in px, the node's size, as a node component gets no plot height.
